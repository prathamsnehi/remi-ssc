# Remi

## Impact Statement

> ✨ turning every face into an unforgettable story ✨

What impact does our app have:

- Improve relationships with those who matter
- Bring back lost context behind familiar faces
- Eliminate the social anxiety of 'getting it wrong': replace hesitation with certainty

## App Flow:

- Home Screen:&#x20;
- - there is a single button that says "scan face", opens up an AR view
  - when in this view and a face is detected, the system tries to detect who this person is
  - - if familiar, shows the basic details of that person
    - otherwise, takes you through the registration process where you could register a person
    - if encounter case where the user accidentally registers one person as two different faces, give option to merge the faces (so it gracefully handles the merging of the memories into one
  - the home screen also contains a horizontal list of recent interactions (the people who i registered / identified recently)
- Friends Profile Screen:
- - shows all the memories associated with a friend
  - shows an ai suggested text that is a conversation starter with the person whose profile you are viewing
- Friends Tab:
- - literally just shows circles with registered people's photos with their names beneath it (and maybe their relation to the person too, or somehow people are organized using relations)
